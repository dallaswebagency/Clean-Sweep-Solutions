Frequently asked questions

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. Our cleaners bring all the necessary supplies and tools. If you prefer particular products or have allergies, let us know and we can use the products you choose.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. All our cleaners are background-checked, insured, and trained to deliver a high level of service, so you can feel secure about hiring us.

How do I schedule or book a cleaning?

Book through the scheduling link on our website or call our customer service team at 780-801-8709. We offer flexible scheduling to fit your needs.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged a fee.

How long will the cleaning take?

It depends on the size of the home or office and the type of cleaning requested. A standard home cleaning typically takes 2 to 3 hours, and we can give you a more accurate estimate after discussing your needs.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No, it's up to you. Some clients give us access while they're away and others stay home. If you won't be there, we can arrange a secure way for our cleaners to enter and leave.

What happens if I am not satisfied with the cleaning?

We aim for 100% satisfaction. If you're unhappy for any reason, contact us within 24 hours and a cleaner will return to address your concerns at no additional charge.

How do I pay for the service?

Payment is made online through Jobber by debit or credit card. You pay a down payment when booking and the balance after the service is completed. You may need a Jobber account to process payment.
